THE LAST LIGHT

"A comic you can play, light you can manipulate, and a story you can't trust."

Theme 1: COMIC
Theme 2: TWIST
Theme 3: LIGHT

HOW TO PLAY

  • W A S D or ARROWS : Move Lumi (the living spark)
  • SPACE : Unleash Light Flare (Kindles lamps & freezes dark shadows)
  • E or MOUSE CLICK : Interact / Rotate mirrors / Talk to characters
  • ★ Spend light carefully to reveal hidden ink, solve optical mirror puzzles, and uncover the truth.
